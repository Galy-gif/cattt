## 完整提示词

Create ONE original app icon / mascot brand mark for a gentle, low-pressure cat companion app, using the attached examples ONLY as stylistic references. Reference 1 (orange FURRRRY cat): learn the bold flat silhouette, charming funny expression, simple graphic economy and sense of spontaneous movement, but DO NOT copy the cat's outline, posture, face arrangement, radiating marks or text. Reference 2 (nine hand-drawn brands): learn warm naive graphic charm, slightly wonky organic shapes and economical facial features. Reference 3 (brand collection): learn memorable playful character design. Ignore all lettering, captions, watermarks and interface in references.
Draw a new compact, full-bodied apricot-orange cat sitting in an endearingly lopsided listening pose, with its entire body gently leaning to one side as though curious about what you are saying. Its head flows into a plump short body as one simple soft block, two uneven small pointed ears, two very short planted feet, one front paw resting against its cheek, and a single bold curved tail tucked beside the body. A simple visible pose, few parts, no tiny anatomy. Two small cream oval eyes with tiny dark-brown pupils looking gently sideways/upward, and a minute off-center nose. The expression is dorky, attentive and relaxed rather than a grin or pleading. Not a big-head portrait: show the whole little creature and its expressive weight and posture. Design a strong unique silhouette, slightly asymmetric and organically hand-shaped, not mathematically perfect or geometric. Make the paw-on-cheek gesture readable with ONE short cream negative-space cut, not lots of contour lines.
Flat spot colors only: vivid but warm tangerine/apricot orange (around #F47740) for the cat, warm off-white #FAF8F3 full-bleed background, tiny dark-brown eye pupils. Large central single motif occupying about 65-70% of square canvas with comfortable margins, no outer border. The character itself is the complete logo; NO typography, no brand name, no letters, no captions, no signature. No extra objects, hearts, sparkles, sound rays, floor lines or badges. No 3D, no fur texture, no ceramic, no photographic shading, no gradients, no shadows. Avoid corporate monoline marks, curled spirals, abstract cat-head symbols, symmetry and generic pet-shop clip art. Flat, warm, playful, deliberately a little odd, exceptionally well art-directed. Square 1024x1024. One finished icon only, not a grid or a moodboard.
