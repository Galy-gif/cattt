# 图标创意候选

用户要求上一轮候选更有创意。本轮沿用扁平手绘感，将产品中的记忆、陪伴与低压力表达融入造型。三张由内置imagegen独立生成，均待用户选择，未替换App资源。

- [A 记忆夹](A-memory.png)：书签猫与纸页，记住说过的小事。
- [B 留个位](B-seat.png)：尾巴伸成空着的小座位，邀请来坐坐。
- [C 慢半拍](C-comma.png)：逗号形小猫，表达可以停一下、慢慢说。

当前为创意方向稿，尚未完成色差平整、矢量化和小尺寸精修。尺寸与哈希见[source.json](source.json)。不取代现有3D角色基准。

## 完整提示词

### A 记忆夹

Design ONE original, genuinely clever and adorable mascot brand mark for a low-pressure AI cat companionship app. Attached images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: naive charming flat color-block characters, wonky organic hand-cut silhouettes, playful editorial brand illustration. Do not reproduce any reference logo, lettering or cat pose. A simple memorable visual idea must be inseparable from the character's silhouette. Warm apricot orange and a tiny amount of dark brown on a warm white full-bleed background, at most one small muted blue accent if called for. Bold readable shapes, very few parts, tiny expressive face, endearing plump proportions, slightly asymmetric hand-drawn geometry. Flat spot colors, no 3D, gradients, fur, shadows, textures, circles framing it or rounded app-icon frame. No words, letters, captions, brand names, watermarks, mockup sheet or multiple variants. One complete centered symbol filling about 65-70% of a square 1024x1024 canvas. Friendly curious dorky expression, not huge staring eyeballs, not a corporate abstract monoline logo, not a realistic cat. The icon should be cute FIRST, conceptual SECOND. IDEA: a cat is ALSO a bookmark, a friend that remembers where you left off. Design a soft short orange ribbon-bookmark character whose top is a little cat head with two tiny ears, lower end resolves into two short feet instead of a sharp ribbon V. It flops sleepily over the upper corner of ONE small warm-cream page, tiny paws draped on either side, a fat curved tail pokes sideways. The page is only a single simple folded-corner silhouette with one muted blue tiny dash, not an illustrated book, no writing. The cat hugs the page corner like a lazy kitten. Fuse cat and bookmark into one compact clever form, not separate cat sitting beside a book. Slightly slanted, charming unexpected proportions; use creamy negative space to show the simple page fold.
