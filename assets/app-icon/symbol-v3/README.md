## 完整提示词

Design ONE exceptionally simple, memorable brand symbol for a low-pressure AI cat companion app. This is a clean graphic identity mark, not an illustration. The brand stands for a stable companion with its own life, gentle continuity, and closeness that leaves room.
Create an original abstract curled-cat monoline emblem: one confident thick flowing ribbon makes an almost closed asymmetrical loop, incorporating two small softly pointed ears in the upper-left contour and a short elegant inward curling tail. It should read as a relaxed curled cat at a glance but remain an abstract iconic logo, not a cat portrait. A deliberate small opening in the loop creates breathing room. Beautiful negative space and optical balance are the primary design features. One integrated shape, very few turns, no ornamental details. The ear geometry must feel designed into the contour, not stuck on. Rounded terminals and subtle organic asymmetry, substantial stroke weight so it survives at 24px. Avoid concentric spirals or multiple nested rings, avoid resembling an at-sign.
Exactly two flat colors: symbol muted burnt coral #B45138, background warm off-white #F5F5F3. No other colors, no lighting, no texture, no material, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no beveled or embossed effects. No eyes, nose, mouth, whiskers, paw prints, heart, stars, house, window, landscape, badges, typography or text. Do not show a logo presentation board or multiple variants. One centered symbol occupying about 66% of a square full-bleed 1024x1024 canvas, generous breathing space, no outer frame and no rounded square tile. Mature, friendly, warm, distinctive graphic design, premium Japanese modernist brand-mark discipline. Deliver only the finished artwork.
