## 共用提示词

The attached Tuanzi image is the only style and proportion baseline. Draw a different cat in exactly the same drawing system. Match the 1:1.4 head-to-body ratio, clearly large head, eyes about 30% of face width and below the face midline, pale-yellow almond eye sockets, half-lidded side gaze, unequal dark bean pupils, no catchlights, low small downturned mouth, pink inner ears, soft cheek blush and long straight whiskers. Match the coloured-pencil and crayon grain, warm-brown variable-weight outlines and soft fur-tuft silhouette. Show exactly one cat in a centered front-sitting full-body pose on a plain warm-cream background. No text, props, scenery, floor line, border, watermark, extra views or expression sheet.

## 奶牛猫

Chinese domestic cow cat; about 75% warm charcoal black and 25% white. White appears in a deliberately asymmetric arrangement: narrow blaze along one side of the nose bridge, white muzzle and chin, continuous white chest area, white paw tips, one or two small flank patches and a short white tail tip. No symmetrical tuxedo. Shorter, tighter silhouette than Tuanzi, with slightly taller ears.

## 黑猫

Chinese domestic black cat; warm charcoal black throughout with restrained midnight-blue and smoky-brown dry-brush for volume. No white markings anywhere. Same head-to-body ratio, but the leaner family silhouette: taller ears, slightly longer legs and a long S-curved tail thick at the base. Keep round cheeks and full haunches. Chest is shown only by fur direction and lighter charcoal; yellow eye sockets remain clearly readable.

## 三花

Chinese domestic calico; roughly 55% vanilla cream, 25% honey orange and 20% warm charcoal black in a few large asymmetric blocks. No speckles or tortoiseshell blending. One side of the face is mainly orange, the opposite upper face partly charcoal, with cream muzzle and chest; two or three large unequal body blocks and a mixed cream-orange-charcoal tail. Slightly rounder pear-shaped body, shorter legs and medium fluffy hooked tail.

## 首轮验收

- 三张都沿用团子的半眯侧视、无眼神光、低位小嘴和暖棕彩铅系统。
- 奶牛猫靠不对称黑白分区、略高耳和紧实轮廓识别。
- 黑猫没有白斑，耳更高、腿略长、尾巴更长，整体最瘦长但仍保留圆脸与厚胯。
- 三花使用大块不对称橙／黑／奶油分区，身体更梨形，短腿和混色尾巴清楚。
