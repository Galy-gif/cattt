# Revision 2 实际提示词

内置 imagegen 实际调用一次，唯一图参考为 canonical 正面图；未引用旧图标。

Use case: logo-brand. Generate a single flat monochrome Tuanzi cat silhouette icon study, optimized FIRST for 24x24 px recognizability. Reference image is canonical identity only: chunky heavy pear body, very short feet, large irregular head and unequal ear angles. No copied texture.
Make ONE large master silhouette on exact solid cream #EFE6D2. Fill cat entirely honey orange #E3A05C. No shading, texture, outlines, face, holes for eyes, stripes, fur tufts, text or props.
Critical silhouette requirement: LONG BROAD S-SHAPED TAIL clearly separate on LEFT side of body. Tail starts at left lower haunch, goes outward left, reverses through a deep rounded bend, then reverses again at top to a bulbous outward tip. Two exaggerated opposite-direction bends: unmistakable capital-S rhythm, NOT C/J hook or a nearly straight wavy stick. Tail thickness about 12% of entire silhouette width, empty cream channel between tail and torso at least 10% width except attachment at base. Tail height about 60% of cat height, ending at cheek level. This spacing and broad bends must survive 24px reduction. Reserve the left third of total icon width for tail and negative space.
Body extremely squat broad and round, little neck indentation, haunches broadest, tiny feet. Head slight tilt; left ear angles outward and right ear upright, easily differentiated by silhouette. Organic asymmetric masses, no perfect circles/ellipses/mirror symmetry.
Whole cat centered in a square composition with only 6% outside margin. Deliver just one high-resolution raster master; actual 24,48,180 pixel outputs will be mechanically exported and checked afterward.
