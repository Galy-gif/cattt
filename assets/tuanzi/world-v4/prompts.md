## nest

Use case: illustration-story. Production sprite atlas for a 2D iOS cat courtyard. Reference image 1 is the approved orange cat identity and wax-crayon with subtle felt material. Draw this EXACT cat, round head, small triangular ears, dark bead eyes, tiny almost invisible warm-brown mouth, orange chest and belly, three forehead stripes, ringed tail. NOT a realistic cat or 3D toy. Create precisely 16 separate cat-only frames in a regular 4 columns x 4 rows grid, reading left to right top to bottom. Square 2048x2048. Every cell equally sized with generous 40px padding and completely separated. All cats face RIGHT. Keep head diameter and body mass constant across ALL frames; crouching changes pose not size. Truly transparent alpha background, no checkerboard pattern, no ground shadow, no scene, props, water, text, labels, grid lines. Each frame full body uncut. Smooth incremental animation, no unrelated poses. Frames 1-3: standing looking up, then small crouch then deepest loaded crouch. Frames 4-8: hind feet pushing off, torso stretching upward diagonally right with front paws reaching, ascent, knees tuck, forepaws arrive together at invisible elevated rim. Frames 9-13: front paws planted on that same invisible rim, chest pulls forward, hind legs bend and lift, belly slides over rim, all four paws crouch on rim. Frames 14-16: settle in supported crouch, lie down with head raised, head lowers onto forepaws. Preserve same head size in final resting pose. No nest pictured, only cat.
