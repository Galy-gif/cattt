# 第二批 · 其余五张场景

**用法**：在画完蘑菇屋的同一个对话里，**把蘑菇屋那张图作为参考图附上**，然后整段发下面的内容。

---

```
Here is the first scene of this world, already approved. It is the STYLE
REFERENCE for everything that follows.

Match it exactly on all of these, by looking at it rather than by any numbers:
- the exact green of the grass, the hills and the treeline
- the exact height of the horizon line where the treeline meets the grass
- the sky colour and how much of the frame it occupies
- the direction and softness of the light
- the coloured-pencil grain, the density of the clover and small flowers
  covering the ground, and the weight and colour of the hand-drawn outlines
- the cat's build, colour and markings

Now draw the remaining FIVE scenes of the same garden.

Output them as FIVE SEPARATE full-size images, one per scene, each 853 x 1844
portrait. Do NOT combine them into a grid or a contact sheet.

These five must look like five views of the SAME garden on the same afternoon.
Someone should be able to put all six side by side and believe they could walk
between them.

RULES THAT CARRY OVER FROM THE FIRST IMAGE

The cat is the same cat: chunky and round, broadest at the haunches, LARGE
head at about 1 : 1.4 head-to-body, thick coat with the silhouette broken by
short soft fur tufts. Honey orange #E3A05C with a darker #C9823F, a continuous
vanilla-cream chest ruff running from under the chin to the belly, three short
forehead stripes, soft broad body stripes, a long ringed tail. Where its eyes
are open they are pale yellow almond sockets, HALF-LIDDED, gaze angled to one
side, dark bean-shaped pupils, NO catchlights, reading as mildly aloof and
self-possessed, never wide-eyed adorable. It is about 8% of the image height:
simplify the face to two dark eye dots at that size, but keep the proportions
-- large head, thick body. Detail shrinks, proportion does not.

The cat is always touching or inside something, never standing alone on open
ground.

Things that GROW cover the ground freely -- grass, clover, wild flowers, small
mushrooms, ferns, moss -- everywhere including the foreground, with NO contact
shadows. Things that are PLACED are few, each one something the cat can use,
each with a soft contact shadow. Never scatter household objects outdoors: no
books, teacups, tables, stools, brooms, watering cans, crates or buckets on the
grass. Those live indoors.

Keep the top 8% and bottom 12% of each frame visually quiet for interface.
No text, no numbers, no interface, no human figure anywhere.

---

SCENE 2 — THE TREE

A large tree fills the right two thirds of the frame, its trunk thick and
textured with real dark values, its canopy reaching into the upper third.

A teardrop-shaped woven nest hangs from a LOW branch, low enough that a cat
could climb in. The cat is inside it, only its head, front paws and the tip of
its ringed tail showing over the rim, looking down.

Placed, and nothing more: a round woven cat bed on the ground among the roots;
a short scratching post leaning against the trunk; a pile of fallen autumn
leaves at the base in warm orange, gold and olive.

Growing: grass and clover around the roots, ferns on the shaded side, two small
mushrooms, moss on one root.

Behind: treeline and hills glimpsed past the trunk. Dappled light on the ground
under the canopy, painted as soft flat shapes, not as a lighting effect.

---

SCENE 3 — THE CAT'S RESTING SPOT

A quiet corner of the lawn, in open daylight.

A woven straw mat lies on the grass with a soft moss-green cushion on it. The
cat is loafing on the cushion, front paws tucked under, eyes half closed, tail
curled around its body.

Placed, and nothing more: a round woven basket beside the mat with a folded
blanket half spilling out; a rolled blanket resting against it.

Growing: grass tufts and clover all around and into the foreground, a small
patch of wild flowers, one or two mushrooms.

Behind: the mushroom house small in the distance on the left, the big tree
small on the right, so this corner is clearly INSIDE the same garden. A low
wooden picket fence runs across the far background, hills and sky above it.

---

SCENE 4 — THE TALL GRASS

A patch of tall meadow grass at the edge of the garden, seen close up.

Tall grasses, reeds and wild flowers fill the lower two thirds of the frame,
drawn as many individual blades and stems, layered pale behind to darker in
front. Some seed heads and dandelion clocks stand above the rest.

The cat is half hidden among the grass, only its head, ears and the tip of its
ringed tail visible above the blades, looking out at the viewer. The grass in
front of it partly OVERLAPS its body, so it reads as being inside the grass,
not standing behind it.

Placed, and nothing more: one flat stone at the left, half swallowed by grass.

Growing: this whole scene is growing things. Clover and small white and yellow
flowers through the grass, two mushrooms low down, a butterfly or a few
floating seeds in the air.

Behind: the tops of the grass give way to open lawn, then a low picket fence,
a treeline, hills and sky.

---

SCENE 5 — THE POND

The pond fills the lower half of the frame.

Still water, its far edge softened by a grassy bank, its near edge coming
toward the viewer. The deep part of the water goes genuinely dark. Lily pads
float on the surface, cattails stand at the left bank. THREE OR FOUR FISH ARE
VISIBLE under the water, drawn simply.

The cat sits on a flat stone at the water's edge, side on, its long ringed tail
hanging down toward the water, watching the fish. Its eyes are open here:
half-lidded, angled down at the water.

Placed, and nothing more: two flat stepping stones at the bank, one mossy rock.

Growing: reeds and cattails, ferns, grass tufts along the bank and into the
foreground, small white and yellow wild flowers, a mushroom or two.

Behind: the far bank rising into a treeline, hills, sky. The pond sits in the
lowest part of the garden, so the banks rise gently toward both sides.

---

SCENE 6 — ON THE FENCE

The garden fence, seen from inside the garden, with the world beyond it.

A low wooden picket fence runs across the middle of the frame, weathered wood
with hand-drawn irregular picket tops and two horizontal rails behind them.
It is the boundary of the garden.

The cat sits ON TOP of the fence, balanced on the flat rail between two
pickets, side on, its long ringed tail hanging straight down. It is looking
OUT, away from the garden, ears forward. This is the only scene where the cat
faces away from home.

Placed, and nothing more: a small wooden mailbox on a post just inside the
fence, its body coral red, its little flag down.

Growing: tall grass and wild flowers crowding the base of the fence on both
sides, looser and taller on the outside than the tended inside, clover and a
couple of mushrooms in the foreground, one climbing vine winding up a picket.

Beyond the fence: open meadow, a dirt path curving away, a treeline, hills and
sky. The world outside is painted slightly lighter and less detailed than the
garden, so the fence clearly separates here from there.
```

---

## 出来之后怎么核

把六张并排看，只看三件事：

1. **地平线是不是同一高度**（树丛与草地的交界）
2. **草的绿是不是同一个绿**，有没有哪张偏黄或偏亮
3. **猫是不是同一只**，头身比有没有在某张缩水

哪张不对就单独重出那一张，参考图仍然是蘑菇屋。**不要因为一张不对就整批重来。**
