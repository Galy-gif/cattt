# 猫猫 App · 四个核心场景 · 独立提示词

思路变更：**不做一张长卷，每个地点画成一张完整竖屏。**镜头跟着猫走到哪儿，就显示哪一张。这也正是世界层九姿态那张图里已经成型的东西，把每个小场景往外扩成整屏。

用法：先贴【通用块】，再贴其中一个【场景块】。一次只做一个场景。

---

## 通用块（每张都要）

```
Paint ONE complete illustration. Do not assemble it from separate cut-out
objects. It must read as a single scene drawn by one hand.

FORMAT
Mobile portrait, 853 x 1844. Keep the top 8% and bottom 12% visually quiet
for interface. No text, no UI, no numbers anywhere.

STYLE
Hand-drawn 2D children's-book illustration. Coloured pencil and crayon grain
inside the colour areas, denser at the centre of a shape and looser toward the
edges. Flat colour underneath. No smooth gradients, no airbrush, no glow, no
drop shadows, no 3D rendering, and no uniform paper texture laid over the
whole image.

LINE
Every contour drawn by hand in dark warm brown #6B5B4A, NEVER pure black.
Line weight varies within a stroke: heavier along undersides and where forms
overlap, tapering on top edges. No perfect circles, no perfect ellipses, no
mirrored shapes. If anything looks made with a compass or a vector tool,
redraw it by hand.

PALETTE — use these values, do not invent intermediate tones
sky #CFE3EA   far hills #C3D5C2   mid hills #AAC5A8   treeline #8FAF8E
ground far #C6D9C4   ground mid #B0C8AD   ground near #9BB899
water #9FC0C2   wood #B39C86   line and darks #6B5B4A
the cat #E3A05C with a darker tone #C9823F and a vanilla cream chest
accent coral #D8705C, at full strength, on less than 3% of the area.
The greens are SAGE, not olive and not khaki. The image must contain real
darks: trunks, doorways, window recesses and deep water go to #6B5B4A or
darker. Ground gets darker toward the viewer, never lighter.

THE CAT
One small orange tabby, about 8% of the image height. Simplified at this size:
two dark eye dots, no whiskers, no facial stripes. Identity comes from the
silhouette, the ear angle and the long ringed tail. It is the only strongly
saturated warm thing in the frame, so it reads at a glance with no outline or
glow. It is always touching or inside something, never standing alone on open
ground.

PLACED versus GROWING — the rule that decides everything
GROWING things cover the ground freely: grass tufts, clover, wild flowers,
small mushrooms, ferns, moss. They appear all over, including the foreground,
with NO CONTACT SHADOWS, because they grew there. They are what makes the
ground feel alive.
PLACED things are few, and every one must be something the CAT can use:
somewhere to sleep, hide, climb, scratch, eat, drink or play. Each sits with a
soft contact shadow, because someone put it there.
NEVER scatter household objects on the grass. No books, teacups, tables,
stools, brooms, watering cans, crates, buckets or sign posts outdoors. Those
belong indoors.

NEVER include: text, numbers, counters or badges; yellowed or aged paper,
stains, speckle noise; thick glass, heavy shadows, plastic or clay look; a
generic anime cat face; any human figure.
```

## 场景一 · 蘑菇屋

```
SCENE — the mushroom house, seen from the front across a small patch of lawn.

The house stands in the middle third of the frame and is about 34% of the
image height: a coral #D8705C cap with soft cream spots, a cream wall, a
rounded wooden door and one round window. Through the open door and the
window you can see a little of the INSIDE: the warm glow of a fireplace, the
corner of a sofa, a small shelf. That is where the household lives.

The cat is loafing on the roof of the house, paws tucked, eyes closed.

Placed, and nothing more: two small bowls, food and water, on the ground right
against the doorway; a bundle of firewood leaning on the wall beside the door.

Growing: grass tufts around the base of the house and scattered across the
lawn, clover, a few wild flowers, two or three small mushrooms, all the way
into the foreground.

Behind: a low treeline, distant hills, open sky. A narrow stone path comes
from the bottom of the frame to the door, with grass growing between the
stones.
```

## 场景二 · 树区

```
SCENE — the big tree, seen from below and to the side.

A large tree fills the right two thirds of the frame, its trunk thick and
textured with real dark values, its canopy reaching into the upper third.

A teardrop-shaped woven nest hangs from a LOW branch, low enough that a cat
could climb in. The cat is inside it, only its head, front paws and the tip of
its ringed tail showing over the rim, looking down.

Placed, and nothing more: a round woven cat bed on the ground among the roots;
a short scratching post leaning against the trunk; a pile of fallen autumn
leaves at the base, in warm orange, gold and olive.

Growing: grass and clover around the roots, ferns in the shade on the shaded
side, two small mushrooms, moss on one root.

Behind: treeline and hills, glimpsed between the trunk and the frame edge.
Dappled light on the ground under the canopy, painted as soft flat shapes, not
as a lighting effect.
```

## 场景三 · 池塘

```
SCENE — the pond, filling the lower half of the frame.

Still water in #9FC0C2, its far edge softened by a grassy bank, its near edge
coming toward the viewer. The deep part of the water goes genuinely dark.
Lily pads float on the surface, a few cattails stand at the left bank.
THREE OR FOUR FISH ARE VISIBLE under the water, drawn simply.

The cat sits on a flat stone at the water's edge, side on, its ringed tail
hanging down toward the water, watching the fish.

Placed, and nothing more: two flat stepping stones at the bank, one mossy
rock.

Growing: reeds and cattails, ferns, grass tufts along the bank and into the
foreground, small white and yellow wild flowers, a mushroom or two.

Behind: the far bank rising into a treeline, distant hills, sky. The ground
around the pond is the lowest part of the world, so the banks rise gently
toward both sides of the frame.
```

## 场景四 · 休息处

```
SCENE — a quiet corner of the lawn where the cat rests, in open daylight.

A woven straw mat lies on the grass with a soft moss-green cushion on it. The
cat is loafing on the cushion, front paws tucked under, eyes half closed,
tail curled around.

Placed, and nothing more: a round woven basket beside the mat, empty, with a
folded blanket half spilling out; a rolled blanket resting against it.

Growing: grass tufts and clover all around and into the foreground, a small
patch of wild flowers, one or two mushrooms.

Behind: the mushroom house small in the distance on the left, the big tree
small on the right, so this corner is clearly INSIDE the same garden. A low
wooden picket fence runs across the far background. Distant hills and sky
above it.
```

## 出图与验收

一次只做一个场景，一次出 3 到 4 版。满意的存为该场景的 canonical，之后所有变体以它作 reference image。

四张必须互相看得出是同一个院子：**同一条地平线高度、同一组地面色、同一个光照方向**。第四张里刻意让蘑菇屋和大树以远景出现，就是为了把四个地点在视觉上缝起来。

出完发我，我这边跑数值体检：地面色相 100–120°、天空 185–210°、水与草色相差 ≥50°、地面由远及近单调变暗。

## 之后再加的场景

摇篮、院门与邮箱、晾衣绳角落。等这四张的画风定死了再扩，那时是照着 canonical 加，不会漂。
