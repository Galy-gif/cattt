## 契约：什么统一，什么可以不同

这是三只能被认成「同一个世界的猫」而不是三张无关插画的唯一保证。

**必须统一，一个数都不能动：**

头身比 1:1.4（头明显偏大）· 眼睛占脸宽约 30%、眼中心落在脸水平中线以下 · 半眯眼、视线偏向一侧 · **无眼神光** · 小嘴偏低、嘴角微微向下 · 粉色内耳 · 柔和腮红 · 长直胡须 · 彩铅蜡笔叠色质感 · 手绘变粗细的暖棕线 `#6B5B4A`

**允许不同，身份就靠这些：**

毛色与花纹 · 毛长（短毛／长毛）· 耳朵角度与高度 · 尾巴长短粗细 · 体块胖瘦一档

**为什么这么切**：头身比和眼位是「可爱」的全部来源，一只一个比例就会失控，回到逐只调参的老路。而辨识度其实是靠**轮廓感**做出来的——黑猫显瘦长、橘猫显敦实，不需要真的改头身比。

## 通用块（三只都要，前面附上团子定稿图）

```
The attached image is Tuanzi, the approved orange tabby. It is the STYLE
BASELINE. Draw a different cat in exactly the same drawing system.

MATCH EXACTLY, do not vary:
- head-to-body ratio 1 : 1.4, the head clearly large
- eyes about 30% of face width, centred below the horizontal midline of the face
- pale yellow almond eye sockets, HALF-LIDDED, gaze angled to one side, dark
  bean-shaped pupils, the two not identical
- NO eye catchlights
- small mouth low on the face, corners turned gently down
- pink inner ears, soft blush on both cheeks, long straight whiskers
- coloured pencil and crayon layering, visible grain, never airbrushed
- hand-drawn outlines in warm brown #6B5B4A, never black, weight varying
  within each stroke, heavier on undersides
- the silhouette broken all over by short soft fur tufts
- reads as mildly aloof and self-possessed, never wide-eyed adorable

Single front-sitting pose, centred, on a warm cream background.
No text, no props, no background scenery.
```

## 奶牛猫

```
[通用块]

THIS CAT: a Chinese domestic cow cat (black and white).

Coat: about 75% warm charcoal black, with white in a DELIBERATELY ASYMMETRIC
arrangement — a narrow white blaze running down one side of the nose bridge,
white muzzle and chin, a small white chest bib, white paw tips, one or two
small white patches on the flank, and a short white tail tip.
Do NOT draw a symmetrical tuxedo pattern. The asymmetry is the identity.

Build: same 1:1.4 proportions, short-haired so the silhouette is a little
tighter than Tuanzi's, ears a touch taller.

The cream chest ruff of the baseline becomes the white chest bib here: still
one continuous area from under the chin, not an oval patch.
```

## 黑猫

```
[通用块]

THIS CAT: a Chinese domestic black cat.

Coat: warm charcoal black throughout, with restrained midnight-blue and smoky
brown dry-brush used only to describe volume. NO white chest, NO white muzzle,
NO white socks, no white markings anywhere.

Build: same 1:1.4 proportions, but read as the leaner one of the family —
taller ears, slightly longer legs, a long S-curved tail thicker at the base.
Round cheeks and full haunches are kept so it never reads bony.

Because there is no white chest, the chest area is described by fur direction
and a slightly lighter charcoal, not by a colour patch. The yellow eye sockets
carry more weight here than on the other cats — keep them clearly readable
against the dark coat.
```

## 三花

```
[通用块]

THIS CAT: a Chinese domestic calico.

Coat: roughly 55% vanilla cream white, 25% honey orange, 20% warm charcoal
black, in a few LARGE asymmetric blocks — not fine speckles, not tortoiseshell
blending. The pattern is fixed and must stay identical in every future drawing
of this cat.

Build: same 1:1.4 proportions, a slightly rounder pear-shaped body, shorter
legs, a medium fluffy hooked tail.

The chest is naturally cream here, continuous from under the chin as in the
baseline.
```

## 验收

三只加橘猫并排看，只核三件事：

1. **头身比是否一致** —— 把四张缩到同一高度，头的大小应该几乎一样
2. **眼睛是否同一套语法** —— 半眯、视线偏侧、无眼神光、瞳孔非正圆
3. **能不能一眼分清是四只不同的猫** —— 如果只能靠颜色分辨，说明轮廓差异不够，需要加大耳角、尾形和体块的区别

哪只不对就单独重做那一只，参考图仍是团子。

## 一个产品提醒

**三花几乎都是母猫**（三花必母是遗传决定的），橘猫则以公猫居多。如果创建流程里外观和性别、名字有关联，懂猫的用户会注意到。不用现在处理，但别让默认值互相打架。
