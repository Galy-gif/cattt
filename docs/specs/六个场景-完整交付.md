# 猫猫 App · 六个核心场景 · 完整出图文档

这份文档是自包含的，不需要任何其他背景，也不需要参考图。可以整个粘贴给出图模型。

**怎么用**：把第一到第四节（背景、画风、团子、摆放规则）连同第五节里的**其中一个**场景块一起发过去，一次只画一个场景。第一张画蘑菇屋，满意之后把那张图作为参考图附上，再画其余五张。

---

## 一、这是什么

一款猫咪陪伴 App 的世界场景。用户认领一只属于自己的猫，它住在一个有栅栏的小院里，过自己的日子。用户打开 App，镜头落到猫当前所在的地点。

这个产品明确**没有**任务、打卡、等级、金币、喂养压力。所以画面里不能出现任何计数、进度条、收集册的暗示。院子里的东西都应该是「已经在那儿的」，不是「等你来打理的」。

猫的名字是**团子**，一只中华田园橘猫。

一共六个地点，每个地点画成一张独立的竖屏。它们属于同一个院子，必须看得出是同一个地方。

---

## 二、画风

手绘二维绘本插画。彩色铅笔与蜡笔的颗粒感在色块内部，中心密、边缘散。底下是平涂色。

**不要**：平滑渐变、喷枪、发光、投影特效、三维渲染、整张图罩一层均匀纸纹。

### 线条

所有轮廓手绘，颜色 `#6B5B4A` 深暖棕，**绝不用纯黑**。同一根线要有粗细变化：形体下缘和交叠处加粗，上缘收细。

**不许出现正圆、正椭圆、镜像对称的形状。**任何看起来像圆规或矢量工具画出来的东西都要重画。这是最容易出错的一条。

### 配色（严格使用，不要自行发明中间色）

```
天空      #CFE3EA
远山      #C3D5C2
中山      #AAC5A8
近树丛    #8FAF8E
地面·远   #C6D9C4
地面·中   #B0C8AD
地面·近   #9BB899
水        #9FC0C2
木头      #B39C86
线与暗部  #6B5B4A
猫        #E3A05C，暗部 #C9823F，胸口香草奶油色
强调色    #D8705C（珊瑚红），全画面面积不超过 3%
```

绿色是**鼠尾草绿**，不是橄榄色，也不是卡其色。**如果地面看起来发黄，就是错的。**

画面必须有真正的暗部：树干、门洞、窗凹、深水处都要压到 `#6B5B4A` 或更暗。地面由远及近逐渐变暗，**绝不能反过来**。

## 三、团子长什么样

这只猫的形象是定稿过的，六张图里必须是同一只。下面是完整描述。

### 体型

**厚实、圆润、敦厚**，最宽处在后半身。不是瘦长的猫，也不是球形的胖猫，是那种毛厚、坐下来会摊开一点的家猫。

**头很大**，头与身体的比例约 **1 : 1.4**，接近幼猫的比例。这是它显得可爱的主要来源，不要把头画小。

毛很厚，**剪影到处被短而软的毛簇打破**，尤其在脸颊、胸口和后腿，不是干净利落的边缘。

### 脸

**这张脸就是这个角色，不要把它画甜。**

- 淡黄色的杏仁形眼眶，**眼睛半眯着**，上眼睑压下来盖住眼球上部约三分之一
- 视线**偏向一侧**，不看镜头
- 深色的豆形瞳孔，两只不完全一样
- 出来的感觉是**有点不在意、自顾自的、不谄媚**。不是难过，不是生气，也不是睁大眼睛的萌
- **绝对不要眼神光。**半眯的眼形本身就在表达，加高光反而假
- 嘴很小，位置偏低，**嘴角微微向下**
- 粉色内耳，两颊有柔和的橙色腮红
- 长而直的深棕色胡须

### 花纹

额头三道短竖纹。身上是柔和的宽体纹，不是细密的虎斑。**长尾巴上有环纹**。

**胸口一片香草奶油色的绒毛**，从下巴一路连到腹部，用松散的簇状笔触画。它必须是**连续的一片**，不是一块椭圆色块，不是围兜，不是独立的一坨。

### 材质

彩色铅笔和蜡笔叠色。有可见的铅笔颗粒。柔和，但绝不是喷枪那种平滑。

### 在这六张图里怎么画

每张图里的猫约占画面高度 **8%**。这个尺寸下五官必须简化：**只有两个深色眼点**，不画胡须、不画脸上的条纹、不画眼眶。

但**体型比例不能跟着缩水**——还是大头、厚身、宽后半身。缩小的是细节，不是比例。

这个尺寸下身份靠三样东西传达：**剪影、耳朵角度、长长的环纹尾巴**。

它是全画面唯一饱和的暖色，所以不需要描边或光晕就能一眼看到。

**猫永远在碰着或待在某个东西里面，绝不单独站在空地中央。**

### 英文版（可直接并入提示词）

```
THE CAT — this is a locked character design, the same cat in all six images.

BUILD: chunky, round, heavy-bodied, broadest at the haunches. Not a slim cat
and not a ball-shaped fat cat: a thick-coated house cat that spreads a little
when it sits. The head is LARGE, head-to-body about 1 : 1.4, near-kitten
proportions. Do not shrink the head. The coat is thick and the silhouette is
broken all over by short soft fur tufts, strongest at the cheeks, chest and
haunches.

FACE — this expression IS the character, do not sweeten it:
pale yellow almond eye sockets, HALF-LIDDED, the upper lid covering the top
third of the eye; the gaze angled to one side, not at the viewer; dark
bean-shaped pupils, the two not identical. It reads as mildly aloof and
self-possessed. NOT sad, NOT angry, NOT wide-eyed adorable.
NO EYE CATCHLIGHTS — the half-lidded shape carries the expression alone.
A small mouth, low on the face, corners turned gently down. Pink inner ears,
soft orange blush on both cheeks, long straight dark brown whiskers.

MARKINGS: three short vertical forehead stripes; soft broad body stripes, not
fine tabby striping; ring markings along the long tail. A continuous
vanilla-cream chest ruff running from under the chin down to the belly, drawn
with loose tufted strokes — one continuous area, NOT an oval patch, NOT a bib,
NOT a separate blob.

COLOUR: honey orange #E3A05C with a darker tone #C9823F.
MEDIUM: coloured pencil and crayon layering, visible grain, soft but never
airbrushed.

AT THE SIZE USED HERE (about 8% of the image height) simplify the face to TWO
DARK EYE DOTS only — no whiskers, no facial stripes, no eye sockets. But keep
the PROPORTIONS: still a large head, thick body, broad haunches. Detail
shrinks, proportion does not. At this size the identity is carried by the
silhouette, the ear angle and the long ringed tail.
```

## 四、摆放规则（决定成败的一条）

院子里的东西分两类，画法完全不同。

**长出来的**：草丛、三叶草、野花、小蘑菇、蕨类、苔藓。它们可以铺满地面，包括最前景，**没有接触阴影**，因为它们是长在那儿的。它们是让地面显得活的东西，铺得多是对的。

**摆上去的**：数量很少，而且每一样都必须是**猫用得上的**——能睡、能躲、能爬、能抓、能吃喝、能玩。每样都有一圈柔和的接触阴影，因为是有人放的。

**绝不要在草地上撒生活杂物。**书、茶杯、桌子、凳子、扫帚、洒水壶、板条箱、水桶、告示牌，这些一律不出现在户外。它们属于屋里。

## 五、六个场景

每张都是 **853 × 1844 竖屏**。上方 8% 和下方 12% 保持视觉安静，留给界面元素。**画面里不要有任何文字、数字或界面。**

一次只做一个场景。

### 场景一 · 蘑菇屋

```
The mushroom house seen from the front across a patch of lawn.

The house stands in the middle third of the frame, about 34% of the image
height: a coral #D8705C cap with soft cream spots, a cream wall, a rounded
wooden door and one round window. Through the open door and the window you can
see a little of the INSIDE: the warm glow of a fireplace, the corner of a sofa,
a small shelf. That is where the household lives.

The cat is loafing on the roof, paws tucked, eyes closed.

Placed, and nothing more: two small bowls, food and water, on the ground right
against the doorway; a bundle of firewood leaning on the wall beside the door.

Growing: grass tufts around the base of the house and scattered across the
lawn, clover, a few wild flowers, two or three small mushrooms, all the way
into the foreground.

Behind: a low treeline, distant hills, open sky. A narrow stone path comes from
the bottom of the frame to the door, with grass growing between the stones.
```

### 场景二 · 树屋

```
The big tree, seen from below and to the side.

A large tree fills the right two thirds of the frame, its trunk thick and
textured with real dark values, its canopy reaching into the upper third.

A teardrop-shaped woven nest hangs from a LOW branch, low enough that a cat
could climb in. The cat is inside it, only its head, front paws and the tip of
its ringed tail showing over the rim, looking down.

Placed, and nothing more: a round woven cat bed on the ground among the roots;
a short scratching post leaning against the trunk; a pile of fallen autumn
leaves at the base in warm orange, gold and olive.

Growing: grass and clover around the roots, ferns on the shaded side, two small
mushrooms, moss on one root.

Behind: treeline and hills glimpsed between the trunk and the frame edge.
Dappled light on the ground under the canopy, painted as soft flat shapes, not
as a lighting effect.
```

### 场景三 · 猫窝

```
A quiet corner of the lawn where the cat rests, in open daylight.

A woven straw mat lies on the grass with a soft moss-green cushion on it. The
cat is loafing on the cushion, front paws tucked under, eyes half closed, tail
curled around its body.

Placed, and nothing more: a round woven basket beside the mat, with a folded
blanket half spilling out; a rolled blanket resting against it.

Growing: grass tufts and clover all around and into the foreground, a small
patch of wild flowers, one or two mushrooms.

Behind: the mushroom house small in the distance on the left, the big tree
small on the right, so this corner is clearly INSIDE the same garden. A low
wooden picket fence runs across the far background, distant hills and sky
above it.
```

### 场景四 · 草丛

```
A patch of tall meadow grass at the edge of the garden, seen close up.

Tall grasses, reeds and wild flowers fill the lower two thirds of the frame,
drawn as many individual blades and stems, layered from pale behind to darker
in front. Some seed heads and dandelion clocks stand above the rest.

The cat is half hidden among the grass, only its head, ears and the tip of its
ringed tail visible above the blades, looking out at the viewer. The grass
in front of it partly overlaps its body, so it reads as being INSIDE the
grass, not standing behind it.

Placed, and nothing more: one flat stone at the left, half swallowed by grass.

Growing: this whole scene is growing things. Clover and small white and yellow
flowers scattered through the grass, two mushrooms low down, a butterfly or a
few floating seeds in the air.

Behind: the tops of the grass give way to open lawn, then a low picket fence,
a treeline, distant hills and sky.
```

### 场景五 · 池塘

```
The pond, filling the lower half of the frame.

Still water in #9FC0C2, its far edge softened by a grassy bank, its near edge
coming toward the viewer. The deep part of the water goes genuinely dark.
Lily pads float on the surface, cattails stand at the left bank. THREE OR FOUR
FISH ARE VISIBLE under the water, drawn simply.

The cat sits on a flat stone at the water's edge, side on, its ringed tail
hanging down toward the water, watching the fish.

Placed, and nothing more: two flat stepping stones at the bank, one mossy rock.

Growing: reeds and cattails, ferns, grass tufts along the bank and into the
foreground, small white and yellow wild flowers, a mushroom or two.

Behind: the far bank rising into a treeline, distant hills, sky. The ground
around the pond is the lowest part of the garden, so the banks rise gently
toward both sides of the frame.
```

### 场景六 · 栅栏上

```
The garden fence, seen from inside the garden, with the world beyond it.

A low wooden picket fence runs across the middle of the frame, its pickets
weathered #B39C86 wood with hand-drawn irregular tops, two horizontal rails
behind them. It is the boundary of the garden.

The cat sits ON TOP of the fence, balanced on the flat rail between two
pickets, side on, its long ringed tail hanging straight down. It is looking
OUT, away from the garden, ears forward. This is the only scene where the cat
faces away from home.

Placed, and nothing more: a small wooden mailbox on a post just inside the
fence, its body coral #D8705C, its little flag down.

Growing: tall grass and wild flowers crowding the base of the fence on both
sides, taller and looser on the outside than the tended inside, clover and a
couple of mushrooms in the foreground, one climbing vine winding up a picket.

Beyond the fence: open meadow, a dirt path curving away, a treeline, distant
hills and sky. The world outside is painted slightly lighter and less detailed
than the garden, so the fence clearly separates here from there.
```

## 六、六张之间的一致性

六张必须看得出是同一个院子。靠三件事保证：

**同一条地平线高度**（画面高度约 46% 处）、**同一组地面色**（上面配色表里的三档绿）、**同一个光照方向**。

场景三特意让蘑菇屋和大树以远景出现，场景四和场景六都画到同一道栅栏，都是为了把地点在视觉上缝起来。

## 七、怎么迭代

一次只做一个场景，一次出三到四版。

**先做蘑菇屋。**它是家，也最能定调。画风在它身上定死之后，另外五张以它作**图参考**（reference image），不要纯文字复现——纯文字每生成一次就会漂一点，攒到第六张就是另一个世界了。

不满意时**每轮只改一个变量**。要更暗就只说更暗，不要同时提颜色和构图。

---

## 八、禁止项

任何文字、数字、计数器、进度条、徽章。

发黄或做旧的纸、污渍、斑点噪声。

厚玻璃、重投影、塑料感或黏土感。

通用的动漫猫脸。

任何人物。

草地上的生活杂物。
