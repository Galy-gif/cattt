# C1 实际提示词

工具：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`。一张新生成方向图，无角色图输入；未使用 CLI 或修改生成图像内容。

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate ONE polished, high fidelity iOS chat screen concept for a minimalist elegant Chinese cat companionship app. Portrait aspect ratio approximately 9:19.5, target 1024 x 2218 if possible. The image is the screen itself, edge to edge, with no physical phone frame, no external margins, no presentation board, no grid, no annotations, no watermark. Entire screen must be visible including native status bar and bottom home indicator.

Design idea: “a quiet white conversation with a glimpse of the cat’s mushroom home”. Premium restraint and precise iOS proportions. White is overwhelmingly dominant. Near-white background #FAFAF8 with imperceptible soft white tonal variation, not a green or blue colored backdrop. Coral/terracotta accent #C25A3E is the only strong accent, echoing the send button. Neutral charcoal text #303332, neutral grey secondary elements. No tan paper, no heavy grain, no dominant colored wash.

Layout on a 390 x 844 reference screen: native status bar at top with time 21:03, small black dynamic island, Wi-Fi and battery. A clean understated header at y85: slim back chevron left, Chinese name “团子” in modest 16pt type beside it; right-aligned “记录” in small neutral grey type, no underline. Generous margins and coherent alignment.
Five message rows in the upper half, readable 15–16pt PingFang-style Chinese text. Short messages use compact bubbles fitted to text, never large stretched cards. Gentle continuous rounded rectangle corners around 14pt, with all four corners rounded; no tails or sharp corners. Cat bubbles on the left are white with an exceptionally fine light grey boundary and tiny soft downward separation. User bubbles on the right are extremely pale coral #F8EEEA with dark text, no dark red fill. Avoid heavy floating shadows. Comfortable but disciplined padding and spacing.
Exact text in order:
cat: “你回来了。”
user: “今天有点累”
cat, two lines: “嗯。” then “那就先不用讲道理。”
user: “路上看到有人在卖桂花”
cat: “桂花是真的开了吗，我没出去过。”
Maintain the correct left/right roles and copy the Chinese exactly. Last cat bubble may use two lines naturally if needed.

CRITICAL AVATAR CHANGE: next to each cat turn, use the SAME tiny generic cat symbol around 30–32pt. It is NOT a portrait of Tuanzi, NOT a realistic cat, NOT a full body animal, NOT felt or plush. Draw a very simple refined coral monoline cat-head contour: two small pointed ears, a gentle rounded cheek/chin contour, and two or three short whisker strokes on each side, with generous white interior. Minimal geometry, consistent fine stroke weight about 1.3pt at 390px screen width, crisp and calm. Very subtle pale coral in the ears can be used. No thick circular badge, no enclosing button, no large black eyes, no cartoon grin. Whiskers and ears must remain recognizable at this small scale. The symbol sits near the lower left of the corresponding bubble.

BACKGROUND SCENE: in the lower right negative space ABOVE the input area, a SINGLE partial mushroom house silhouette, about one third of the screen width and one sixth of screen height. Its cap and curved walls are suggested with delicate near-neutral warm grey and pale coral contour lines, a tiny arched doorway and one small glowing window. The edges have a very subtle soft coral-white luminous halo, like a faint etched light drawing on white glass. Deliberately incomplete contours fade into the white background and crop slightly at the right edge. This must be visibly recognizable but secondary. Do not turn it into a filled illustration, sticker, 3D toy or fully painted garden. No additional trees, flowers, paw-print decoration, sparkles, stars, glowing dots, fog, rainbow gradients or broad pink flood. Keep all scene contours away from the message text; use the natural lower-page empty space.

Bottom composer fixed close to the bottom safe area: a light white rounded rectangular field about 46pt high with a fine neutral edge, placeholder “说点什么” in subtle grey, a small slim microphone symbol at the right of the field. Beside it a 46pt square coral send button with 12pt corners and a crisp thin white upward arrow. Same height and coherent rounding, restrained shadow. Large comfortable touch target but visually light. White bottom area, black home indicator. No keyboard.

Overall: a beautiful, believable, minimal native iOS product screenshot. The refinement comes from spacing, proportion, coherent line weights, quiet rounded bubbles, one memorable luminous architectural motif and generous white space. Chinese copy must be accurate and readable. Avoid decorative script, huge typography, thick bevels, generic dashboard cards, excessive glass reflections.
```
